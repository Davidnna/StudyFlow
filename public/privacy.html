Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

StudyFlow is a student final-year project. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

1. What we collect

  • Account info: your name and email address (from sign-up, or from Google, Microsoft, or X if you use those to sign in).
  • Study data: the tasks, focus sessions, and settings you create inside the app.
  • We do not collect payment information, location data, or browsing activity outside the app.

2. How it's stored

Your data is stored on your device and synced to Google Firebase (Firestore), which hosts the app's cloud database and authentication. Firebase is operated by Google; see Firebase's own privacy documentation for how they handle data on our behalf.

3. How it's used

Your data is used only to provide StudyFlow's features to you — showing your tasks, your stats, and syncing between your devices. We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

4. Sign-in with Google, Microsoft, or X

If you sign in with Google, Microsoft, or X, we receive your name, email (where the provider shares it), and profile photo from that provider, used only to create and identify your StudyFlow account.

If you sign in with Microsoft, we also fetch your Microsoft profile photo through the basic User.Read permission (we don't read your mail, files, or contacts) and save a small copy of it in your StudyFlow profile.

Profile pictures and a third-party image service: photos you upload yourself, and Microsoft photos, are stored in your StudyFlow profile and are never sent to a third party. An X profile photo is loaded directly from X's image server. Some browsers' privacy settings block that, so if the direct load fails the app may load it once through wsrv.nl, a third-party image proxy. In that case wsrv.nl can see the photo's web address and your IP address. If that also fails, your initials are shown instead.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete account inside the app. This permanently removes your tasks, sessions, and account.
